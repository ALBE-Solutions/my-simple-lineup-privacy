2. Grundsatz: keine Datenübertragung

„My Simple Lineup“ ist ein Werkzeug zum Erstellen und Verwalten von Mannschaftsaufstellungen. Die App funktioniert vollständig offline und verfügt in der veröffentlichten Fassung nicht einmal über die Berechtigung für einen Internetzugriff.

Es gibt kein Nutzerkonto, keinen Server und keine Cloud. Es gibt keine Werbung, kein Tracking, keine Analyse-Tools und keine Erhebung von Gerätekennungen. Wir als Entwickler erhalten diese Daten zu keinem Zeitpunkt und haben keinerlei Zugriff darauf – eine Weitergabe an Dritte ist technisch ausgeschlossen.

3. Welche Daten werden verarbeitet?

Die App verarbeitet ausschließlich Daten, die du selbst eingibst oder auswählst:

Darüber hinaus erhebt die App keine Daten – insbesondere keine Standortdaten, keine Kontakte und keine Nutzungs- oder Absturzstatistiken. Ein Profiling oder eine automatisierte Entscheidungsfindung nach Art. 22 DSGVO findet nicht statt.

2. Core principle: no data transmission

“My Simple Lineup” is a tool for creating and managing team lineups. The app works entirely offline. The published version does not even hold the Android permission required for internet access.

There is no user account, no server and no cloud. There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics and no collection of device identifiers. All data you enter stays exclusively in your device’s private app storage. We as the developer never receive this data and have no access to it whatsoever — passing it on to third parties is technically impossible.

3. What data is processed?

The app processes only data that you enter or select yourself:

  • Player names and shirt numbers
  • Team names
  • Lineups and formations (positions, captain, goalkeeper)
  • Photos you voluntarily choose for teams or players
  • App settings (e.g. accent colour, theme, language)

Beyond this, the app collects no data — in particular no location data, no contacts and no usage or crash statistics. No profiling and no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

Provision is voluntary (Art. 13(2)(e) GDPR): You are under no legal or contractual obligation to enter any data. Without entries you simply cannot create teams and lineups.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the app’s functionality and storing your entries locally so they are still there next time you open the app: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).
  • Selecting photos via the system photo picker: your voluntary consent, which you may withdraw at any time, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. Photos

Photos are selected exclusively through the system photo picker. You decide for each individual action which single image the app may use. The image is stored as a copy in the private app storage and never leaves your device. The app does not request permission to access your entire photo library (e.g. READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).

6. Data about other people

When you enter names, shirt numbers or photos of other people into the app, this happens exclusively on your own device. No upload takes place: this data is not transmitted to us, not to any server and not to third parties. We do not store or process any data about the people you enter.

Which data you enter, and whether you are entitled to do so, is therefore solely your own responsibility — just as it would be if you noted the details in a notes app or on a sheet of paper.

Practical note: if your entries concern underage players, the consent of a parent or legal guardian is generally required. Only use photos with the consent of the people shown, or of their parents or legal guardians.

7. Retention period and deletion

All data remains stored on your device until you delete it yourself. There is no server-side storage and therefore no retention beyond that.

  • You can delete individual players, teams, formations and photos directly in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data completely from your device.

8. Data collected by the app store (Google Play)

When you download the app via Google Play, Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) independently processes data such as your user name, email address, device and payment information. We have no influence over this processing; Google acts as its own controller in this respect. For more information see the Google Privacy Policy.

9. International use

The app is available worldwide. Wherever you live, the same applies: the app collects no data, transmits nothing and stores nothing on any server.

We do not sell your personal information, do not share it and do not disclose it to third parties — neither for payment nor for advertising or analytics purposes. This is not merely a promise: since no data ever reaches us, doing so would not be technically possible for us.
